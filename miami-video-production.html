FAQ

Miami video production questions, answered

How does pricing work for Miami productions?

We scope around crew size, hours on-site, deliverables, and distribution needs. Most bookings land in the three packages above, but we can tailor a quote for unique venues in Brickell or Wynwood. Rates include insured crew, gear, and standard permits so budgeting stays predictable.

What turnaround can we expect?

Reels deliver within 48 hours, with faster same-day selects available for leadership updates or sponsor thank-yous. Final highlight films typically land within a week, and we can mirror fast-turnaround timelines when you need an accelerated schedule.

Where do you operate in Miami?

We cover Downtown, Brickell, Wynwood, Miami Beach, Coral Gables, and the wider South Florida area. For multi-city tours, we coordinate with our crews nationwide so your brand voice and quality remain consistent from Miami to New York or Los Angeles.

Do you handle permits and venue coordination?

Yes. We advise on permits for outdoor shoots, manage COI requests, and align with venue A/V teams to keep compliance simple. If your event mirrors a complex conference setup, we replicate that process locally.

Can you travel for satellite offices?

Absolutely. We’re a Miami video production company with national reach. If your leadership is hosting partner events in Chicago or filming customer stories in Seattle, we bring the same crew standards and playbook you see on this page.
